# Problem brief

People save articles and links to read later — across a browser
extension, a phone share sheet, and pasted URLs — but quickly lose track
of what they saved. There is no way to tell what has already been read,
saved items pile up unsorted, and the same link often gets saved two or
three times from different devices.

When someone finally has time to read, they open a long undifferentiated
list, cannot find the thing they meant to get back to, and give up. The
saved items become a graveyard instead of a queue.

There is no PRD for this yet. This is a bare problem domain.
